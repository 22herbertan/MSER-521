About VWIN

Founded in 1990, the Volunteer Water Information Network (VWIN) is a volunteer-based, surface water monitoring program. Trained volunteers collect water samples from rivers and lakes in the French Broad, Watauga, Catawba, Broad, New, and Little Tennessee River Basins. Samples are then analyzed at EQI's state-certified laboratory for 8 different parameters:

  • pH
  • Turbidity
  • Total Suspended Solids
  • Alkalinity
  • Orthophosphate
  • Nitrates
  • Ammonia
  • Fecal Coliform (select samples)
  • Total Phosphate (select samples)
  • Conductivity
