VWIN Site Ratings

VWIN site water quality scores are calculated from three consecutive years of data, so for example, the 2020 score uses data from 2018, 2019, and 2020. These scores use data from orthophosphate, nitrate-nitrite-nitrogen, ammonia-nitrogen, turbidity, total suspended solids, and conductivity analyses. Letter grades are determined for each parameter and site using grading rules listed below. The grades are assigned an associated value (A=100, B=75, C=50, D=25). A maximum VWIN site score of 100 and a minimum of 25 are possible. Scores for sediment (turbidity and TSS only) and nutrients (orthophosphate, nitrates, and ammonia only) are also calculated.

VWIN ratings are assigned as follows:

  • Excellent: 90-100 VWIN score
  • Good: 80-89 VWIN score
  • Average: 70-79 VWIN score
  • Below Average: 60-69 VWIN score
  • Poor: < 60 VWIN score
